Log to Salesforce

  1. Open an email or meeting in Outlook and click Log to Salesforce on the ribbon.
  2. Sign in to Salesforce the first time.
  3. Pick the person (Contact or Lead) and what it relates to (Account, Opportunity or Case). Use Add to create a missing Contact or Lead.
  4. Choose whether to include the body and which attachments, then click Log.

Emails become completed Tasks, meetings become Events, and attachments are saved as Files on the related record.

Questions? Contact your Salesforce administrator.
